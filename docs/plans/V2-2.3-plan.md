## 1. Architectural Scope & Contract

- **Goal**: Build comprehensive, validated Cube 1.7.x semantic models in `version-two/cube/model/*.js` for all four core business domains (`AnnuityQuotation.js`, `InvestmentAnalysis.js`, `MemberAnalysis.js`, and `SharedDimensions.js`), binding queries against the DuckLake `lake.*` catalog, fixing legacy column mismatches (`member_age` instead of nonexistent `current_age`), defining multi-table join graphs (`dim_member`, `dim_scheme`, `dim_date`), and establishing all standard business measures (`totalAua`, `activeMemberCount`, `quotationCount`, `averageCommissionRate`).
- **POPIA Dimension Masking**: All PII dimensions (`id_number`, `member_id`, `client_name`, `member_nk`) use `compilePiiDimensionSql` so unauthorized roles never read unmasked PII.

## 2. Step-by-Step Implementation Tasks

1. **Author `version-two/cube/model/SharedDimensions.js`**:
   - Define `SharedDimensions`, `DimDate` (`lake.scbi_sdp_mart.dim_date`), `DimMember` (`lake.scbi_cdp_mart.cnf__dim_member`), `DimScheme` (`lake.scbi_cdp_mart.cnf__dim_fund` / scheme metadata), `DimFund`, `DimClient`, `DimEmployer`, `DimPaypoint`, `DimBrokerConsultant`, `DimAnnuityProduct`, and `DimInvestmentProduct`.
   - Fix legacy column bug: bind `memberAge` to `member_age` (never `current_age`).
   - Apply `compilePiiDimensionSql` to `id_number`, `member_id`, `client_name`, and `member_nk`.

2. **Author `version-two/cube/model/AnnuityQuotation.js`**:
   - Bind to `lake.scbi_cdp_mart.cnf__fact_annuity_quotations`.
   - Define joins to `DimDate`, `DimMember`, `DimScheme`, `DimFund`, `DimBrokerConsultant`, and `DimAnnuityProduct`.
   - Define measures: `quotationCount`, `acceptedQuotations`, `quotedMembers`, `acceptedMembers`, `quotedPurchasePrice`, `acceptedPurchasePrice`, `averageCommissionRate` (`avg` over `commission_rate`), `memberConversionRate`, `purchasePriceConversionRate`.

3. **Author `version-two/cube/model/InvestmentAnalysis.js`**:
   - Define `InvestmentAnalysis` (plus legacy aliases `InvestmentsFundamental` and `MemberMonthlyInvestment`) over `lake.scbi_cdp_mart.cnf__fact_member_investment_aua` and `lake.scbi_cdp_mart.cnf__fact_inv_monthly_market_value`.
   - Define joins to `DimDate`, `DimMember`, `DimScheme`, `DimFund`, `DimClient`, `DimEmployer`, and `DimInvestmentProduct`.
   - Define measures: `totalAua`, `totalMarketValue`, `averageCommissionRate`, `distinctClients`, `distinctPortfolios`, `activeMemberCount`.

4. **Author `version-two/cube/model/MemberAnalysis.js`**:
   - Define `MemberAnalysis` (and legacy alias `MemberMonthly`) over `lake.scbi_cdp_mart.cnf__fact_member_investment_aua` joined with `lake.scbi_cdp_mart.cnf__dim_member` (`dim_member`), `dim_scheme`, and `dim_date`.
   - Bind `member_age` accurately (eliminating legacy `current_age`).
   - Define measures: `activeMemberCount`, `totalAua`, `avgAua`, `averageCommissionRate`.
   - Define POPIA-governed dimensions (`id_number`, `member_id`, `client_name`, `member_age`, `member_age_band`, `member_gender`).

5. **Author `version-two/cube/model/index.js` & Wire `version-two/cube/cube.js`**:
   - Provide a schema loader & compiler (`loadAllDomainCubes()`, `compileSemanticQuery(query, securityContext)`, `executeSemanticQuery(driver, query, securityContext)`) that registers all `cube('...', {...})` definitions, validates joins/measures/dimensions, compiles SQL with POPIA masking, and executes sample queries against the embedded DuckLake driver.

6. **Author `version-two/tests/test_v2_2_3_semantic_domain_cubes.py`**:
   - **AC1 (`test_all_four_domain_model_files_load_and_compile`)**: Verifies `AnnuityQuotation.js`, `InvestmentAnalysis.js`, `MemberAnalysis.js`, and `SharedDimensions.js` exist in `version-two/cube/model/` and compile cleanly under Cube 1.7.x schema validation.
   - **AC2 (`test_member_age_replaces_nonexistent_current_age`)**: Verifies `member_age` is bound in `MemberAnalysis.js` and `SharedDimensions.js` and `current_age` is completely absent from `version-two/cube/model/*.js`.
   - **AC3 (`test_primary_facts_and_dimension_joins_defined`)**: Verifies primary facts (`cnf__fact_annuity_quotations`, `cnf__fact_member_investment_aua`) and joins (`dim_member`/`DimMember`, `dim_scheme`/`DimScheme`, `dim_date`/`DimDate`) are registered and resolvable.
   - **AC4 (`test_standard_business_measures_and_sample_queries_execute`)**: Verifies standard business measures (`totalAua`, `activeMemberCount`, `quotationCount`, `averageCommissionRate`) and executes sample queries against all 4 domain cubes through the embedded DuckLake driver.
